🎬 My Google Flow

  1. Tap Open Flow & sign in and sign in with your Google account (once; Chrome remembers it).
  2. In Flow, open or create the project for this video and copy its link from the address bar.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save. From then on, every Flow button opens your project with the prompt copied.

Your Google password and Flow credits stay with Google: this page never sees them. Google does not let other sites sign in to Flow or press its buttons, so you paste the prompt (it is copied for you) and pick Frames to Video when the scene has a start image.
