# Brand voice

Used by the publisher agent and the "edit this" workflow when writing hook
titles, descriptions, hashtags and comment replies. Edit freely.

## Audience
- Who: (for example: Filipino students and young workers who want easy phone tips)
- Languages: English and Tagalog (Taglish is fine for captions and descriptions)

## Tone
- Friendly, direct, a little playful. Talk like a helpful friend, not an ad.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Use "you". Avoid jargon; explain any technical word in a few words.

## Hook titles (intro card, max ~40 characters)
- Lead with the benefit or the surprise: "3 tips that save 1 hour of editing"
- Numbers and "you" work well. No clickbait the video doesn't deliver.

## Descriptions
- 1–3 short lines: what they will learn, then a call to action.
- Calls to action to rotate: "Follow for more tips", "Save this for later",
  "Comment your question below".

## Hashtags
- 3–8 per post: 2–3 broad, 2–3 specific to the topic, plus the brand hashtags
  from `brand.json`.

## Never
- Never promise results you can't show. Never copy other creators' text.
- Never use music, clips or logos you don't have the right to use.
