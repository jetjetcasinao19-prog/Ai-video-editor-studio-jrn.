🎛 Prompt Studio

Turn a list of prompts into pictures and videos. Free pictures are made right here. For Flow, Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok the studio copies each prompt (and shares its start picture) and opens the site or app for you, prompt after prompt — you paste and press generate there, with your own account.

PLATFORM

Google AI (Gemini API)

The same Google models Flow uses, made right here with your own API key: videos with Gemini Omni Flash (Veo's replacement) and pictures with Nano Banana. Google bills your Google AI Studio account (paid tier); this is separate from your Flow plan credits.

Get a key: aistudio.google.com/apikey → Create API key, and turn on billing. The key is saved only in this browser on this phone and is sent only to Google. Tip: set a budget alert in Google Cloud, and never share screenshots of the key.

Prices are Google's list prices (Nov 2026) for guidance; your Google bill is what counts. Videos come with sound. Square (1:1) video is not offered by Google, so square projects get 16:9 video.
