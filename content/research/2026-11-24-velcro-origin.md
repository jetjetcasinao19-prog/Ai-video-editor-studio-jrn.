# Research — The invention of Velcro

Topic type: Inventions / People
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-24

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Swiss engineer George de Mestral noticed burrs stuck to his clothes and his dog's fur after an outing in the mountains (the Jura) | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/velcro-invention), [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/es/web/ip-advantage/w/stories/the-velcro-ip-story), [APS News](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2004/02/death-george-mestral-velcro) |
| Under a microscope he saw tiny hooks that caught on loops in fabric and fur | [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/george-de-mestral), [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/es/web/ip-advantage/w/stories/the-velcro-ip-story), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/velcro-invention) |
| His fastener used one strip with tiny hooks and one with tiny loops; nylon worked better than cotton | [WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/es/web/ip-advantage/w/stories/the-velcro-ip-story), [APS News](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2004/02/death-george-mestral-velcro) |
| He patented it in the 1950s (Swiss filing 1951; granted mid-1950s) | [APS News](https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2004/02/death-george-mestral-velcro), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_de_Mestral), [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/george-de-mestral) |
| "Velcro" combines the French words velours (velvet) and crochet (hook) | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_de_Mestral), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/velcro-invention) |
