# Research — The origin of LEGO

Topic type: Businesses & brands / Toys
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-21

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| LEGO began in carpenter Ole Kirk Christiansen's workshop in Billund, Denmark; during the Great Depression, in 1932, he started making wooden toys | [LEGO.com — The beginning](https://www.lego.com/en-gb/history/articles/b-the-beginning-of-the-lego-group), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/LEGO), [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/ole-christiansen) |
| Early toys included wooden ducks and yo-yos | [LEGO.com — The beginning](https://www.lego.com/en-gb/history/articles/b-the-beginning-of-the-lego-group), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-disastrous-backstory-behind-the-invention-of-lego-bricks) |
| In 1934 the company took the name LEGO, from the Danish "leg godt" ("play well") | [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/ole-christiansen), [Wikipedia — History of Lego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Lego) |
| In March 1942 a fire destroyed the woodworking factory; it was rebuilt | [LEGO.com — Disaster strikes](https://www.lego.com/de-de/history/articles/c-disaster-strikes), [History Hit](https://www.historyhit.com/ole-kirk-christiansen-the-danish-carpenter-who-invented-lego/) |
| In 1949 the company began making plastic "Automatic Binding Bricks", based on interlocking bricks by the British company Kiddicraft | [Wikipedia — History of Lego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Lego), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-disastrous-backstory-behind-the-invention-of-lego-bricks) |
| In 1958 the modern stud-and-tube brick was patented | [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/ole-christiansen), [Wikipedia — Ole Kirk Christiansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ole_Kirk_Christiansen), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/LEGO) |

## Points sources disagree on (kept out of the script)
- Cause of the 1942 fire (short circuit vs. other accounts): script just says it burned.
- Name year (1934 in most sources, 1936 in one): script uses 1934.
- Who holds the 1958 patent (Ole vs. son Godtfred): script says "they patent".
