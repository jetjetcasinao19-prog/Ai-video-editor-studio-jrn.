## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| On October 16, 1846 ("Ether Day"), at Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston, the first public demonstration of inhaled ether as a surgical anesthetic took place | [Wikipedia — Ether Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ether_Dome), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/operating-room-where-anesthesia-first-demonstrated-now-landmark-men-claimed-credit-much-misery-180988842/), [EBSCO](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/safe-surgical-anesthesia-demonstrated) |
| Boston dentist William Morton gave the ether to patient Gilbert Abbott, who had a tumor on his neck | [Wikipedia — Ether Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ether_Dome), [Linda Hall Library](https://www.lindahall.org/about/news/scientist-of-the-day/ether-dome), [NYU Medical Humanities](https://medhum.med.nyu.edu/view/10331) |
| Surgeon John Collins Warren operated, then told the audience: "Gentlemen, this is no Humbug" | [Wikipedia — Ether Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ether_Dome), [Linda Hall Library](https://www.lindahall.org/about/news/scientist-of-the-day/ether-dome) |
| The operating theater became known as the Ether Dome; the event is commemorated as the beginning of modern anesthesia | [Wikipedia — Ether Dome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ether_Dome), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/operating-room-where-anesthesia-first-demonstrated-now-landmark-men-claimed-credit-much-misery-180988842/) |
