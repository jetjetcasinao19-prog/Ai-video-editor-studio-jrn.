# Research — Hannibal's childhood oath and his war on Rome

Topic type: Ancient history (war) / People
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-23

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| At about age nine, Hannibal begged his father Hamilcar to take him on campaign (to Spain); Hamilcar agreed if he swore never to be a friend of Rome — a story Hannibal himself later told (recorded by Polybius; Livy similar) | [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/hannibal/), [Dickinson College Commentaries](https://dcc.dickinson.edu/node/238), [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/hannibal) |
| In 218 BC Hannibal invaded Italy by crossing the Alps with an army and dozens of war elephants | [History.com](https://www.history.com/topics/ancient-rome/hannibal), [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/hannibal), [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/hannibal/) |
| He lost many troops and elephants in the crossing | [Dickinson College Commentaries](https://dcc.dickinson.edu/node/238), [TheCollector](https://www.thecollector.com/hannibal-barca-facts/) |
| In 216 BC he crushed a much larger Roman army at Cannae (often said to be about twice his size) | [History.com](https://www.history.com/topics/ancient-rome/hannibal), [TheCollector](https://www.thecollector.com/hannibal-barca-carthage-enemy/), [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/hannibal/) |
| He never attacked/captured the city of Rome | [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/hannibal/), [History.com](https://www.history.com/topics/ancient-rome/hannibal) |

## Points sources disagree on / left out
- Army sizes and how many elephants survived: script says "dozens of war elephants" and "many… don't survive".
- Exact oath wording and ritual details: script uses the core promise only.
- Why he never attacked Rome is debated: script just states he never took it.
