# Research — The origin of the Hollywood sign

Topic type: History / Places
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-21

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Built in 1923 as a temporary ad reading "HOLLYWOODLAND" for a real estate development in the hills of Los Angeles; expected to last about 18 months | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/90-years-later-8-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-hollywood-sign), [Discover Los Angeles](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/the-hollywood-sign-the-story-of-an-la-icon), [USC Dornsife](https://dornsife.usc.edu/news/?p=4487) |
| It was lit with light bulbs at night | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/90-years-later-8-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-hollywood-sign), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Sign) |
| In 1949 the Hollywood Chamber of Commerce repaired it and removed "LAND" | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Sign), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/90-years-later-8-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-hollywood-sign) |
| By the 1970s it had badly decayed | [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/hollywood-waved-goodbye-an-iconic-old-sign-disarray-1133481), [LAist](https://laist.com/news/la-history/original-hollywood-sign-torn-down-replaced-history) |
| In 1978, donors each paid for one letter of a new sign (Hugh Hefner hosted a fundraiser and took the Y; Alice Cooper paid for an O in memory of Groucho Marx); the new sign was finished in 1978 | [Hollywood Sign Trust](https://www.hollywoodsign.org/history/a-sign-is-reborn-1978), [PBS SoCal / KCET](https://pbssocal.org/kcet-50th-anniversary/november-1978-hollywood-sign-restored), [Ultimate Classic Rock](https://ultimateclassicrock.com/alice-cooper-hollywood-sign/) |
