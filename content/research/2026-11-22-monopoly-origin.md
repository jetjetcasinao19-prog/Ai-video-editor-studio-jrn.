# Research — The origin of Monopoly

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Lizzie (Elizabeth) Magie patented "The Landlord's Game" (US patent 748,626, granted January 5, 1904) | [British Library](https://www.bl.uk/stories/blogs/posts/lizzie-magie-and-the-history-of-monopoly), [UK IPO blog](https://ipo.blog.gov.uk/2023/12/22/monopoly-and-the-inventor-who-was-streets-ahead), [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/the-landlords-game) |
| She designed it to show how landlords get rich while tenants are ruined (a lesson about unfair wealth) | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/monopoly-was-designed-teach99-about-income-inequality-180953630/), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/monopoly-game-inventor-elizabeth-magie), [National Women's History Museum](https://www.womenshistory.org/articles/monopolys-lost-female-inventor) |
| People made homemade versions for years; players in Atlantic City added their own street names | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/monopoly-was-designed-teach99-about-income-inequality-180953630/), [The Strong / Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/8wXRngw0ZZDFLQ) |
| Charles Darrow sold his version to Parker Brothers in 1935, was presented as the inventor, and royalties made him a millionaire | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/monopoly-game-inventor-elizabeth-magie), [Emory Business History](https://scholarblogs.emory.edu/gbsl/this-day-in-business-history-monopoly-patented-pass-go-collect-2021), [National Women's History Museum](https://www.womenshistory.org/articles/monopolys-lost-female-inventor) |
| Parker Brothers bought Magie's patent for $500 | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/monopoly-game-inventor-elizabeth-magie), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/monopoly-was-designed-teach99-about-income-inequality-180953630/), [British Library](https://www.bl.uk/stories/blogs/posts/lizzie-magie-and-the-history-of-monopoly) |
| Her role came to light in the 1970s through economist Ralph Anspach's legal fight over his "Anti-Monopoly" game | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/monopoly-was-designed-teach99-about-income-inequality-180953630/), [National Women's History Museum](https://www.womenshistory.org/articles/monopolys-lost-female-inventor) |
