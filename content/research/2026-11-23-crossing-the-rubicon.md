## Points sources disagree on / left out
- Exact wording of the famous line (Suetonius vs. Plutarch vs. modern readings): script credits Suetonius and uses the common translation.
- Exact date (Jan 10/11): script says "January".
- The 1933 renaming of a river as "Rubicone": not confirmed here; not used.
