# Research — The origin of the Nike swoosh

Topic type: Businesses & brands / People
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-21

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| The Swoosh was created in 1971 by Carolyn Davidson, a graphic design student at Portland State University, where Phil Knight taught accounting | [Nike — Swoosh history](https://about.nike.com/en/stories/nike-swoosh-logo-history), [Wikipedia — Swoosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swoosh), [Portland State University](https://www.pdx.edu/alumni/legendary-design) |
| Knight's company (then Blue Ribbon Sports) needed a logo for its new shoes on a deadline | [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/16286876/how-college-student-carolyn-davidson-created-nike-swoosh), [Nike — Swoosh history](https://about.nike.com/en/stories/nike-swoosh-logo-history) |
| Knight's reaction: "I don't love it, but maybe it will grow on me" (Nike's own page has a slightly different wording) | [Wikipedia — Swoosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swoosh), [Nike — Swoosh history](https://about.nike.com/en/stories/nike-swoosh-logo-history) |
| Davidson billed Knight $35 | [Nike — Swoosh history](https://about.nike.com/en/stories/nike-swoosh-logo-history), [Portland State University](https://www.pdx.edu/alumni/legendary-design) |
| In 1983, Knight gave her a gold Swoosh ring with a diamond and Nike stock | [Wikipedia — Carolyn Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolyn_Davidson_(graphic_designer)), [Portland State University](https://www.pdx.edu/alumni/legendary-design), [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/16286876/how-college-student-carolyn-davidson-created-nike-swoosh) |
