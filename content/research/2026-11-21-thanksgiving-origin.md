## Points sources disagree on (kept out of the script)
- How long she campaigned (17 years, "decades", 36 years) and how many presidents she wrote to (4 or 5). Script names the four NPR lists and gives no year count.
- Secretary of State William Seward wrote the proclamation's text; the script says Lincoln "declares", which is accurate.
- The 1941 law fixing the fourth Thursday is a later chapter; not in this video.
