# Research — The origin of Bubble Wrap

Topic type: Things & ideas / Businesses & brands
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-21

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Invented in 1957 by Alfred Fielding and Marc Chavannes, two engineers | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-bubble-wrap-180971325/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_Wrap_(brand)) |
| It started in Hawthorne, New Jersey | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-bubble-wrap-180971325/), [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/original-purpose-bubble-wrap) |
| They heat-sealed two shower curtains together, trapping air bubbles | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-bubble-wrap-180971325/), [Mental Floss](https://mentalfloss.com/article/13092/bubble-wrap-was-originally-supposed-be-wallpaper) |
| They first tried to sell it as textured wallpaper; it didn't sell | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-bubble-wrap-180971325/), [Core77](https://www.core77.com/posts/79543/Did-You-Know-Bubble-Wrap-was-Invented-in-1957-as-3D-Wallpaper-That-Failed-to-Sell) |
| Next they tried greenhouse insulation; that didn't take off either | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-bubble-wrap-180971325/), [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/original-purpose-bubble-wrap) |
| Their company is Sealed Air Corporation (most sources say 1960) | [Wikipedia — Sealed Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sealed_Air), [MMH](https://www.mmh.com/article/sealed_air_celebrates_55th_anniversary/Sealed_Air) |
| Marketer Frederick W. Bowers saw that IBM was about to ship its new 1401 computer; IBM used the bubbles to protect it, the first big win | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-bubble-wrap-180971325/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_Wrap_(brand)) |
| "Bubble Wrap" is a trademarked brand of Sealed Air | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bubble_Wrap_(brand)) |

## Points sources disagree on (kept out of the script)
- Sealed Air's founding: most say 1960, one says incorporated by 1959. Script says "they started a company" without a year.
- The IBM deal: 1960 or 1961. Script gives no year.
