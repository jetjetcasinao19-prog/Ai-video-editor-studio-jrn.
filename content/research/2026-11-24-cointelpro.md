## Facts (safe to say)
- COINTELPRO ("Counterintelligence Program") was an FBI program (1956–1971)
  to surveil, infiltrate, discredit and disrupt US political and activist
  groups, including civil rights groups.
- Methods documented by the Church Committee include anonymous and fake
  letters, planted rumors, and efforts to create distrust and splits inside
  and between groups.
- In November 1964 the FBI mailed King an anonymous letter (with a tape),
  written as if from a disillusioned Black supporter. It ends "There is only
  one thing left for you to do. You know what it is." King and historians
  read it as urging him to kill himself.
- On March 8, 1971 (the night of the Ali–Frazier "Fight of the Century"),
  activists broke into the FBI office in Media, Pennsylvania, took files and
  mailed them to newspapers. That exposed COINTELPRO; the program was
  formally ended in 1971. The burglars were not publicly known until 2014.
