# Research — Wilson Greatbatch's implantable pacemaker (the "wrong part" story)

Topic type: Inventions (medicine) / People
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-24

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Engineer Wilson Greatbatch, in Buffalo, New York, was building an oscillator to record heart sounds/rhythms in the 1950s | [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/research/about-us/units.host.html/content/shared/smbs/research_highlights/pacemaker.detail.html), [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/wilson-greatbatch), [Heart Rhythm Society](https://hrsonline.org/40th-Anniversary/Wilson-Greatbatch) |
| He installed the wrong component by mistake, and the circuit gave off a steady pulse like a heartbeat | [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/research/about-us/units.host.html/content/shared/smbs/research_highlights/pacemaker.detail.html), [Heart Rhythm Society](https://hrsonline.org/40th-Anniversary/Wilson-Greatbatch), [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/wilson-greatbatch) |
| He saw it could drive a failing heart; with surgeon William Chardack he developed an implantable pacemaker | [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/research/about-us/units.host.html/content/shared/smbs/research_highlights/pacemaker.detail.html), [National Academies memorial tribute](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/18959/chapter/22), [MIT Technology Review](https://cdn.technologyreview.com/s/401186/setting-the-pace) |
| In 1960, the first Greatbatch pacemaker was implanted in a human patient | [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/research/about-us/units.host.html/content/shared/smbs/research_highlights/pacemaker.detail.html), [Lemelson-MIT](https://lemelson.mit.edu/award-winners/wilson-greatbatch) |
| Medtronic licensed the invention | [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/research/about-us/units.host.html/content/shared/smbs/research_highlights/pacemaker.detail.html) |

## Points sources disagree on / left out
- The wrong part: a resistor (most sources, 1956) vs. a transistor (Lemelson, 1958): script says "the wrong part" and "the 1950s".
- Greatbatch's was not the only early implantable pacemaker (others were developed in the same era): script says "one of the first".
