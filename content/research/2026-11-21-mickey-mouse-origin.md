## Points sources disagree on / left out
- How much of the train-ride story is legend: script says the idea came "on the train home", as Disney told it.
- Plane Crazy was made first but didn't find a distributor: left out for length.
- "First cartoon with synchronized sound" is disputed (earlier sound cartoons existed): script only says it had sound.
