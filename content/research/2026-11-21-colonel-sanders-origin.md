## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Harland Sanders began serving meals to travelers at his service station in North Corbin, Kentucky, around 1930, later opening Sanders Court & Café across the street | [Historical marker (HMDB)](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=24612), [Wikipedia — Harland Sanders Café and Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harland_Sanders_Caf%C3%A9_and_Museum) |
