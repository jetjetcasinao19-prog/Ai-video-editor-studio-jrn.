# Trending AI story styles — November 2026

Research for the **Story styles** gallery in the AI Story Studio (`story.html`,
styles in `story-styles.js`). Most sources are blogs from AI-tool companies,
so their numbers are directional, not verified.

## What is working on TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts

| Trend | What it looks like | Evidence | Style in the app |
|---|---|---|---|

## Rules that protect the channel
- **Originality pays, mass-produced slop doesn't.** YouTube and TikTok say AI
  content is monetizable when it is original (your script, voice, direction);
  re-narrated Reddit threads and articles you didn't write are named as formats
  that get demonetized. The writer prompts ask for original stories.
- **Label realistic AI.** TikTok has labeled over 3 billion videos as AI. One
  source says the label alone doesn't cut reach, but you should disclose
  realistic AI footage.
- **No real people's likeness**, no fake news, no political deepfakes (a big
  problem on Philippine Facebook in 2025–2026). The prompts say so.
- **Health and money styles** must not promise cures or returns; they end with
  "ask your doctor" or say it's general information.
