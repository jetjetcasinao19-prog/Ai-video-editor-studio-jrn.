| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| The idea came from Philip Johnston, son of a missionary to the Navajo, one of the few non-Navajos fluent in the language | [National Archives — Prologue](https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2001/winter/navajo-code-talkers.html), [Marine Corps History Division](https://www.usmcu.edu/Research/Marine-Corps-History-Division/People/Navajo-Code-Talkers-in-WWII/) |
