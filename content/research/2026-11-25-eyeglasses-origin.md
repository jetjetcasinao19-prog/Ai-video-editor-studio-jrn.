# Research — The mystery inventor of eyeglasses

Topic type: Inventions (medical / everyday) / History
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-25

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Eyeglasses were first made in Italy around the 1280s–1290 (probably Pisa; some argue Florence or Venice) | [Oxford — Cabinet](https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/node/10901), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2016/03/medieval-eyeglasses-wearable-technology-of-the-thirteenth-century/), [Encyclopedia.com](https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/invention-spectacles) |
| In a sermon in Florence (1305 or 1306), Friar Giordano da Pisa said spectacles had been invented less than twenty years earlier, and that he had met the inventor | [Oxford — Cabinet](https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/node/10901), [Harry Ransom Center (UT Austin)](https://sites.utexas.edu/ransomcentermagazine/2012/04/17/medieval-spectacles/), [Encyclopedia.com](https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/invention-spectacles) |
| The inventor's name is unknown; the later attribution to Salvino degli Armati is considered false | [Encyclopedia.com](https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/invention-spectacles), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2016/03/medieval-eyeglasses-wearable-technology-of-the-thirteenth-century/) |

## Points sources disagree on / left out
- Exact sermon year (1305 vs. 1306): script says "around 1306".
- Pisa vs. Florence vs. Venice: script says "probably in Pisa or Florence".
- Friar Alessandro della Spina, who reportedly made and shared glasses after seeing a pair: left out for length.
- Venice's 1300 glassmaking rule about reading lenses: left out for length.
