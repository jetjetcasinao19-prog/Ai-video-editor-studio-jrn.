# Research — Why the Statue of Liberty is green

Topic type: History / Places ("why is it like this?")
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-22

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| The statue was unveiled October 28, 1886, as a reddish-brown copper statue | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/statue-of-liberty-green-color-change), [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/statue-liberty-different-color/), [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/statue-of-liberty-originally-different-color) |
| Its skin is about 300 copper sheets, roughly as thick as two pennies | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/statue-of-liberty-green-color-change), [Copper Development Association](https://copper.org/education/liberty/liberty_reclothed1.php) |
| Copper exposed to air and moisture slowly forms a green patina; it spread over the statue in the early 1900s | [American Chemical Society](https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/pressroom/reactions/infographics/statue-of-liberty-became-green.pdf), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/statue-of-liberty-green-color-change), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/video/why-is-the-statue-of-liberty-green-k31x0j) |
| In 1906, Congress set aside money to repaint/repair it; there was public outcry (a newspaper called it "sacrilege") and the idea was dropped | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/statue-of-liberty-green-color-change), [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/statue-liberty-different-color/), [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/statue-of-liberty-originally-different-color) |
| The patina protects the copper underneath | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/statue-of-liberty-green-color-change), [American Chemical Society](https://www.acs.org/content/dam/acsorg/pressroom/reactions/infographics/statue-of-liberty-became-green.pdf) |

## Points sources disagree on (kept out of the script)
- When it became fully green (1906 vs. about 1920): script says "year by year".
- The amount Congress set aside ($62,000 vs. $62,800): script doesn't give a number.
