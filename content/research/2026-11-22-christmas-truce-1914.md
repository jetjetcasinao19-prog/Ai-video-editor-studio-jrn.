## Points sources disagree on / left out
- Whether soldiers were punished: sources differ; not used.
- How big/organized the football games were: script says "in some places, they even play soccer".
- Christmas trees with candles on the trenches: often told, not confirmed in these results; not used.
- Earlier local truce on Dec 11 (Essex/Saxon troops): left out for length.
