# Research — The origin of the word "OK"

Topic type: Things & ideas (words)
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-21

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| In the late 1830s, Boston newspapers had a fad of joke abbreviations of misspelled phrases (e.g. "O.W." for "oll wright") | [Wikipedia — OK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OK), [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/wordplay/the-hilarious-history-of-ok-okay), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-birth-of-ok-175-years-ago) |
| On March 23, 1839, the Boston Morning Post printed "O.K." for "oll korrect" (all correct) in a humorous piece | [History.com — This day in history](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/march-23/ok-enters-national-vernacular), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/how-one-man-discovered-the-obscure-origins-of-the-word-ok-180953258/) |
| In 1840, President Martin Van Buren, nicknamed "Old Kinderhook" after his hometown of Kinderhook, New York, ran for reelection; supporters formed O.K. clubs, which spread the word | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-birth-of-ok-175-years-ago), [Wikipedia — OK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OK), [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/50042/whats-real-origin-ok) |
| Van Buren lost the 1840 election, but "OK" survived | [Wikipedia — OK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OK), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-birth-of-ok-175-years-ago) |
| Columbia scholar Allen Walker Read traced the origin in articles in American Speech in the 1960s | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/how-one-man-discovered-the-obscure-origins-of-the-word-ok-180953258/), [Wikipedia — OK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OK) |
| OK is one of the most widely used words in the world | [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/the-birth-of-ok-175-years-ago), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/how-one-man-discovered-the-obscure-origins-of-the-word-ok-180953258/) |

## Points sources disagree on (kept out of the script)

- Some people still argue other origins (see [List of proposed etymologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_proposed_etymologies_of_OK)); script follows the mainstream (Read / Merriam-Webster) account.
