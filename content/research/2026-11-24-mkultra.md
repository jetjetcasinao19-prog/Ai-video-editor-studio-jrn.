# Research — Project MKUltra (government dark psychology)

Niche: psychology ("dark psychology" series, government angle). Format: 25–40 s Short.

## Sources
- US Senate (1977). *Project MKULTRA, the CIA's Program of Research in
  Behavioral Modification.* Joint hearing of the Select Committee on
  Intelligence and the Subcommittee on Health and Scientific Research,
  August 3, 1977.
  https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/sites/default/files/hearings/95mkultra.pdf
- Church Committee final report (1976), Book I, on CIA drug testing.
- John Marks, *The Search for the "Manchurian Candidate"* (1979), built on the
  documents he obtained through a Freedom of Information Act request.

## Facts (safe to say)
- MKUltra was a CIA program approved in 1953 (Director Allen Dulles), led by
  chemist Sidney Gottlieb, studying drugs (especially LSD) and other methods
  for interrogation and behavior control. Related work ran until about 1973.
- Some tests used people who did not know they were being drugged
  ("unwitting subjects").
- Operation Midnight Climax: CIA-funded safehouses in San Francisco (and New
  York) where men were secretly given LSD and observed through one-way mirrors.
- In 1973, CIA Director Richard Helms ordered the MKUltra files destroyed.
- In 1977, about 20,000 documents that had survived (stored with financial
  records, so they weren't destroyed) were found after a FOIA request and led
  to the August 1977 Senate hearing.

## Don't say
- Don't claim it "worked" as mind control: the Senate record does not show
  that. Don't add theories (e.g., celebrity or modern "mind control" claims).
- Don't use the CIA seal or logo (federal law limits use of the seal).
