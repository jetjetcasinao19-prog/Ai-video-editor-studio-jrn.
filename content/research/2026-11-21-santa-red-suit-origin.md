# Research — Where Santa's look came from (did Coca-Cola make Santa red?)

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| The 1823 poem "A Visit from St. Nicholas" describes him as "chubby and plump, a right jolly old elf", with a sleigh and eight tiny reindeer | [Wikipedia — A Visit from St. Nicholas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Visit_from_St._Nicholas), [Coca-Cola Company — Five things](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/company/history/five-things-you-never-knew-about-santa-claus-and-coca-cola) |

| Red-suited Santas existed in other art and ads before Coca-Cola | [The Ferret fact check](https://theferret.scot/fact-check-coca-cola-red-santa-claus-christmas/), [Ripley's](https://www.ripleys.com/stories/coca-cola-santa) |
| From 1931, Coca-Cola's illustrator Haddon Sundblom painted Santa for its ads, until 1964 | [Coca-Cola Company — Haddon Sundblom](https://www.coca-colacompany.com/about-us/history/haddon-sundblom-and-the-coca-cola-santas), [Atlanta History Center](https://www.atlantahistorycenter.com/blog/a-look-inside-coca-cola-at-christmastime/) |
