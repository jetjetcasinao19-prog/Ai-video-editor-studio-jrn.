## Points sources disagree on / left out
- Total Greek force (about 7,000 vs. other estimates): script says "about 7,000".
- Persian army size: Herodotus' huge numbers are rejected; script says "a massive army" only.
- "Molon labe" ("come and take them") is reported much later (Plutarch); not confirmed here; not used.
- The Thebans' role (forced to stay, then surrendered): left out for length.
