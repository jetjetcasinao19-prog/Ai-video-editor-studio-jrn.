# Research — The origin of Play-Doh

Topic type: Things & ideas (toys) / Businesses & brands
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-21

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Play-Doh began in the 1930s in Cincinnati, Ohio, as a wallpaper cleaner made by the soap company Kutol | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-play-doh-180973527/), [The Strong Museum of Play](https://www.museumofplay.org/blog/the-history-of-play-doh-good-clean-fun/) |
| Coal-heated homes left soot on wallpaper; the doughy putty was rolled over walls to lift it off | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-play-doh-180973527/), [The Strong Museum of Play](https://www.museumofplay.org/blog/the-history-of-play-doh-good-clean-fun/) |
| After World War II, homes moved from coal to oil and gas heat, and washable vinyl wallpaper arrived, so sales dropped | [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-play-doh-180973527/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh) |
| Kay Zufall, a nursery school teacher and Joe McVicker's sister-in-law, read that wallpaper cleaner could be used for modeling, tried it with her class, and suggested selling it as a toy | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-play-doh-180973527/), [Reminisce](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/reminisce/20200701/282750588898576) |
| Rainbow Crafts was formed in 1956 to make and sell it as Play-Doh | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh), [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/innovation/accidental-invention-play-doh-180973527/) |
