# Research — The CIA's Contra manual (1983–84)

Niche: psychology ("dark psychology" series, government angle). Format: 25–40 s Short.

## Sources
- *Psychological Operations in Guerrilla Warfare: The CIA's Nicaragua Manual*,
  with essays by Joanne Omang and Aryeh Neier (Vintage Books, 1985). Full
  English text of the manual ("Operaciones sicológicas en guerra de
  guerrillas"). Also on the CIA FOIA Reading Room: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/
- Associated Press and *The New York Times*, October 1984 (leak and reaction).
- House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, report on the manual
  (December 1984).

## Facts (safe to say)
- Written in 1983 by a CIA contract employee for the Contras, rebels fighting
  Nicaragua's Sandinista government. Became public in October 1984.
- It advised the "selective use of violence" to "neutralize" carefully
  chosen officials such as judges, police and state security officials.
  Critics read "neutralize" as assassinate (banned for US agencies by
  Executive Order 12333, 1981).
- It suggested hiring professional criminals for selective "jobs".
- It described leading demonstrators into confrontation with authorities to
  provoke riots or shootings that would kill one or more people, who would
  become "martyrs" for the cause.
- It caused a national scandal: it came up in the October 21, 1984
  presidential debate, and Congress investigated; several CIA officials were
  disciplined.

## Safe framing
- Say the manual "suggested" / "talked about"; quote "neutralize" and
  "martyr" as its words.
- "Here's the trick" = martyrdom: a death stirs more anger than words.
