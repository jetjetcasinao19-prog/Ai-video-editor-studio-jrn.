## Points sources disagree on / left out
- The taxi company reportedly made money but was dropped over driver labor costs, so the script says "none of it lasted", not "it failed".
- "Love hotels" are only alleged and don't fit the brand; left out.
- Ultra Hand sales (1 million vs. 1.2 million): script says "over a million".
- Year of the factory visit (about 1966) varies; script says "the mid-1960s" only for the money trouble.
