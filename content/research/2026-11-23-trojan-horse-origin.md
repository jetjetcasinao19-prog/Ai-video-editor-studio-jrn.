# Research — Where the Trojan Horse story comes from

Topic type: Ancient history (war) / Myths & legends
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-23

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| The Trojan Horse is not in Homer's Iliad, which ends before Troy falls | [Wikipedia — Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/trojan-horse-gullibility), [Interesting Literature](https://interestingliterature.com/2019/05/why-the-trojan-horse-almost-certainly-wasnt-a-horse-real-origins-history/) |
| The Odyssey mentions it only briefly | [Wikipedia — Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse), [Interesting Literature](https://interestingliterature.com/2019/05/why-the-trojan-horse-almost-certainly-wasnt-a-horse-real-origins-history/) |
| The detailed story is told by the Roman poet Virgil in the Aeneid | [Wikipedia — Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse), [Britannica — The Trojan War](https://www.britannica.com/place/Troy-ancient-city-Turkey/The-Trojan-War) |
| In the Aeneid, the priest Laocoön warns: "I fear the Greeks, even bearing gifts" (Timeo Danaos et dona ferentes) | [Wikipedia — Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse), [History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/trojan-horse-gullibility) |
| There is no archaeological proof of a horse; ancient writers themselves suggested it was really a siege engine of some kind (other theories: a symbol of an earthquake) | [Interesting Literature](https://interestingliterature.com/2019/05/why-the-trojan-horse-almost-certainly-wasnt-a-horse-real-origins-history/), [Wikipedia — Trojan Horse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trojan_Horse), [Discovery](https://www.discovery.com/exploration/City-Of-Troy-Was-Real) |

## Points sources disagree on / left out
- Which god sent the serpents that killed Laocoön (Poseidon vs. Apollo): not used.
- The earthquake theory is disputed: left out of the script.
