# Research — How the Heimlich maneuver began (1974)

Topic type: Inventions & discoveries (medicine) / People
Audience: US viewers, 16–34
Date: 2026-11-25

## Checked facts (used in the script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Dr. Henry Heimlich published an informal article on his anti-choking method in the journal Emergency Medicine in June 1974 ("Pop Goes the Café Coronary") | [History.com](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/june-1/heimlich-maneuver-for-choking-published), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/dr-heimlich-got-maneuver), [1974 article (PDF)](https://www.gwern.net/doc/biology/1974-heimlich.pdf) |
| The method used upward pressure below the ribs to force air from the lungs and push out the blockage | [History.com](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/june-1/heimlich-maneuver-for-choking-published), [1974 article (PDF)](https://www.gwern.net/doc/biology/1974-heimlich.pdf) |
| At the time it had been tested only on dogs; he wrote it might not carry over to humans | [1974 article (PDF)](https://www.gwern.net/doc/biology/1974-heimlich.pdf), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/dr-heimlich-got-maneuver), [Today I Found Out](https://www.todayifoundout.com/?p=54668) |
| Within weeks, a Seattle-area newspaper reported a choking rescue using the method (June 1974) | [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/dr-heimlich-got-maneuver), [History.com](https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/june-1/heimlich-maneuver-for-choking-published) |
| An August 1974 JAMA editorial first called it the "Heimlich maneuver" | [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/dr-heimlich-got-maneuver) |

## Points sources disagree on / left out

- How to perform it, and today's first-aid guidance (which also includes back blows in many programs): not taught in the video — point viewers to certified first-aid training.
- Later controversies about Heimlich's other claims: left out.
