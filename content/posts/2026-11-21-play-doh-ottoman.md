- **Summary:** Play-Doh was invented to clean your walls. In the 1930s in
  Cincinnati, Ohio, homes burned coal for heat, and the soot turned wallpaper
  black. A soap company called Kutol sold a soft, doughy putty that rolled the dirt
  right off. Then, after World War II, homes switched from coal to gas and oil.
  Washable wallpaper arrived, and nobody needed the putty anymore. Then Kay Zufall,
  a nursery school teacher in the family, read that kids were modeling with
  wallpaper cleaner. She tried it with her class, and the kids loved it. Her idea?
  Sell it as a toy. In 1956, it was reborn with a new name: Play-Doh. A wall
  cleaner that became a childhood classic.
