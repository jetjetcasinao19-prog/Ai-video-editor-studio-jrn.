- **Fact check:**
  - Sources: US Senate, Joint Hearing before the Select Committee on Intelligence and the
    Subcommittee on Health and Scientific Research, "Project MKULTRA, the CIA's Program of
    Research in Behavioral Modification," August 3, 1977; Church Committee final report
    (1976); John Marks, *The Search for the "Manchurian Candidate"* (1979).
  - MKUltra was approved in April 1953 by CIA Director Allen Dulles. It researched drugs
    (especially LSD), hypnosis and other methods for interrogation and "behavior
    modification"; some subjects were unwitting. "Mind control program" is the common
    shorthand; it never produced reliable mind control.
  - The San Francisco safehouse operation (nicknamed "Midnight Climax," run by agent
    George White from the mid-1950s into the 1960s) used women paid by the program to
    bring men to apartments where they were given LSD without knowing and observed
    through one-way mirrors. A similar safehouse ran in New York.
  - In 1973, CIA Director Richard Helms ordered the MKUltra files destroyed. In 1977, about
    20,000 documents were found that had survived because they were stored in a records
    center for financial files; they came out through a Freedom of Information Act
    request and led to the August 1977 Senate hearing.
  - The death of Frank Olson (1953) after being given LSD without his knowledge is part of
    this history; it is not mentioned in the video.
  - The INKBLOT look is a style choice, not a claim about Rorschach or a psychological test.
