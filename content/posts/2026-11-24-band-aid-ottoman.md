- **Hook (first line of the video):** "The Band-Aid was invented out of love."
- **Summary:** Around 1920, Earle Dickson, a cotton buyer at Johnson & Johnson, saw that his wife, Josephine, kept cutting
  herself at home and that ordinary gauze and tape would not stay on her active hands. He laid small squares of gauze along a
  long strip of surgical tape (covered with crinoline so the tape would not stick to itself) so she could cut off a piece and
  dress her own wound. He showed it to his employer, and Band-Aid brand adhesive bandages went on sale in 1921, handmade at
  first.

**Description:**
Around 1920, Johnson & Johnson cotton buyer Earle Dickson noticed that his wife, Josephine, kept cutting her fingers at home and that regular gauze and tape would not stay put. So he laid small squares of gauze along a long strip of surgical tape, and she could snip off a piece and dress her own cuts. His employer saw the potential, and Band-Aid brand adhesive bandages went on sale in 1921, handmade at first.
