**Title:** The One-Word Reply That Gave Us "Laconic" #shorts
**Description:**
Have you ever wondered why "laconic" means using very few words? It is named after Laconia, the region of southern Greece where Sparta stood. The word comes from the Greek Lakonikos, "of Laconia," because the Spartans were known for terse speech. The best-known example is a story told by the Greek writer Plutarch. As the story goes, King Philip II of Macedon, the father of Alexander the Great, sent the Spartans a threat. Their answer was one word: "If." Whether it happened exactly this way is uncertain, and the wording of the threat differs between retellings, but the word it inspired is in every dictionary.
