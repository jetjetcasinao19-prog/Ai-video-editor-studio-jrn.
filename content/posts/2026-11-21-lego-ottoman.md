- **Summary:** LEGO started with a carpenter who was running out of work. In 1932, in
  Billund, Denmark, the Great Depression hit and work dried up for Ole Kirk Kristiansen.
  So he started carving wooden toys instead: wooden ducks and yo-yos. In 1934, he named
  his company LEGO, from the Danish words "leg godt," meaning "play well." In 1942, his
  factory burned to the ground, and he rebuilt. In 1949, the company started making a new
  kind of toy: plastic bricks that stack together, inspired by a British toy. In 1958,
  they patented the brick we know today, with tubes inside that make it click and hold. A
  carpenter's side project, now found in homes around the world.
