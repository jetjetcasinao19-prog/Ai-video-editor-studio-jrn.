  - Sources: US Army 6th Psychological Operations Battalion materials on "Ghost Tape
    Number 10" (c. 1970); the Smithsonian and NPR coverage of the recordings; SGM Herbert
    A. Friedman's psyop histories (psywarrior.com).
  - Operation Wandering Soul used recordings made with South Vietnamese voice actors:
    eerie music and sound effects, plus "ghost" voices of dead soldiers urging their
    comrades to go home. They were played at night from helicopters and ground
    loudspeakers, mainly around 1970.

    cultural belief, not as superstition to mock.

    reports say troops sometimes fired at the speakers or the helicopters.
