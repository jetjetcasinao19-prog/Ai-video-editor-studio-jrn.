- **Hook (first line of the video):** "A rocket scientist invented this toy by accident in his bathroom."
- **Summary:** Lonnie Johnson, an engineer who worked on NASA missions, was
  building a cooling system that runs on water. He tested a homemade nozzle on
  his bathroom sink, and a blast of water shot across the room. That would make
  an amazing water gun. He built a prototype, but it took years to get a toy
  company on board. He kept pushing, and finally a company called Larami said
  yes. It hit stores as the Power Drencher, then got a new name: the Super
  Soaker. As stated in the video, Larami sold about 27 million of them in three
  years. Message: a science experiment soaked a whole generation.
