- **Hook (first line of the video):** "Basketball started with two peach baskets."
- **Summary:** Basketball started with two peach baskets. It was winter in
  Springfield, Massachusetts, too cold to play outside. A young gym teacher, James
  Naismith, got a challenge from his boss: invent an indoor game to keep his
  students active. Fun fact: Naismith wasn't even American. He was Canadian. He
  grabbed a soccer ball, nailed two peach baskets ten feet up and wrote 13 rules.
  Then the class played the very first game: December 21st, nine players a side.
  The final score? One to nothing. In those early games, every time someone scored,
  a janitor had to climb a ladder to get the ball back. Those 13 rules became one
  of the most popular sports on Earth.
