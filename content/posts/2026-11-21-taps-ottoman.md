You may have heard Taps was found in a fallen soldier's pocket. That story is a legend. Here is the real one. In July 1862, during the Civil War, Union General Daniel Butterfield's brigade was camped at Harrison's Landing in Virginia. Its official name was Extinguish Lights, but soldiers called it Taps. Before long, it was played at military funerals, and by 1891 it was a standard part of them. A bedtime bugle call that became America's song of goodbye.
