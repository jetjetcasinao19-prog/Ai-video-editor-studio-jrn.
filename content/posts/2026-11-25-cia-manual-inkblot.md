- **Fact check:**
  - Sources: the manual *Operaciones Sicológicas en Guerra de Guerrillas* (CIA, 1983;
    English translation published by Random House, 1985, as *Psychological Operations in
    Guerrilla Warfare*); US House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence report on
    the manual (December 1984); contemporary AP and *New York Times* coverage (October
    1984).
  - The manual was written in 1983 by a CIA contractor for the Contras (the FDN), and its
    existence became public in October 1984 when AP reported on it.
  - It advised the "selective use of violence" to "neutralize carefully selected and
    planned targets, such as court judges, police and state security officials." Critics
    called it the "murder manual."
  - It suggested using hired agitators to lead demonstrators into clashes with the
    authorities, provoking a death to create a "martyr" for the cause (paraphrased; the
    exact wording varies between translations).
  - It became a national scandal during the 1984 presidential campaign. President Reagan
    ordered an investigation, Congress held inquiries, and a few mid-level CIA officers
    were disciplined. The manual is often discussed alongside the Boland Amendment
    restrictions on aid to the Contras.
