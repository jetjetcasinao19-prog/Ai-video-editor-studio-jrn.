- **Summary:** Nintendo is 137 years old, and it started with playing cards. In
  Kyoto, Japan, Fusajiro Yamauchi opened a small shop making Japanese playing
  cards. Decades later, a new boss, Hiroshi Yamauchi, wanted more than cards.
  Nintendo tried a taxi company and instant rice, and none of it lasted. By the
  mid-1960s, Nintendo was close to going broke. Then the boss visited a card
  factory and spotted a young engineer, Gunpei Yokoi, playing with an arm he
  built for fun. He told him to turn it into a toy. The Ultra Hand sold over a
  million. That engineer later led the team behind the Game Boy, a card shop that
  became a gaming giant. Message: a company on the edge was saved by a toy built
  for fun.
