- **Fact check:**
  - Sources: Mumford, M. D., Rose, A. M., & Goslin, D. A. (1995). *An Evaluation of
    Remote Viewing: Research and Applications*. American Institutes for Research (for
    the CIA); declassified CIA STAR GATE records (CIA CREST reading room).
  - US remote-viewing research began in the early 1970s (CIA-funded work at the
    Stanford Research Institute), and the Army/DIA unit at Fort Meade, Maryland ran under
    names including Gondola Wish, Grill Flame, Center Lane and Sun Streak before STAR
    GATE (1991–1995). "Over 20 years" and "about $20 million" are the commonly cited
    totals.

    used to guide intelligence operations, and the program was ended. The reviewers
    (Jessica Utts and Ray Hyman) disagreed on the lab results; Hyman pointed to vague
    descriptions that judges could read meaning into.

    effect: in 1949, Bertram Forer gave students the same vague "personality profile"
    (partly from a horoscope book) and they rated it as highly accurate.
