# Bubble Wrap Was Meant to Be Wallpaper — Ottoman miniature short

- **Video:** `animate/projects/bubble-wrap/out/bubble-wrap-ottoman.mp4`
  (59.0 s, 9:16, 1080x1920, How it Started brand: word-by-word captions, logo +
  @HOWITSTARTED-YT1 handle, closing card "Every Legend Has a Beginning" and
  "Subscribe for more origin stories")
- **Hook (first line of the video):** "This was supposed to go on your walls."
- **Summary:** Two engineers, Alfred Fielding and Marc Chavannes, heat-sealed two
  plastic shower curtains together in Hawthorne, New Jersey, and trapped air
  inside as tiny bubbles. Their idea was textured wallpaper. Nobody bought it.
  Greenhouse insulation flopped too. They didn't quit: they started Sealed Air.
  Then a marketer on their team spotted a problem. IBM was about to ship its new
  1401 computer, and computers were fragile. The bubbles were perfect, and IBM
  became their first big customer. Today you know it as bubble wrap. Message:
  a failed idea can find its real purpose.
- **Edit notes:** a 2D animated short drawn entirely in code, in the style of an
  Ottoman miniature painting (illuminated manuscript page, marbled paper margins,
  gold skies, figures in kaftans and turbans; Sealed Air shown as a tughra
  monogram with a seal; the IBM computer sails on a galley; a Piri Reis-style
  world map at the end). Music and sound effects are generated by code. Captions
  from Groq Whisper, name corrected to "Marc" (Chavannes). On-screen dates: 1957,
  1960, IBM 1401.
