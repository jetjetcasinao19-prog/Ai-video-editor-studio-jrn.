- **Video:** `animate/projects/post-it-notes/out/post-it-notes-ottoman.mp4`

- **Hook (first line of the video):** "This glue was a failure. Now it's on desks everywhere."
- **Summary:** A 3M scientist, Spencer Silver, tried to make a super strong glue
  and made the opposite: a glue so weak it peels off and sticks again. Years
  later, his co-worker Art Fry was singing in his church choir, and his bookmarks
  kept falling out of his hymn book. Then it clicked: what if the bookmark could
  stick? 3M tested the sticky pads as Press 'n Peel, and they flopped. But 3M
  didn't quit. In 1978 it flooded Boise, Idaho with free samples, and people
  loved them. Two years later the pads hit stores across America as Post-it
  Notes. Message: a failed glue can become a desk icon.
