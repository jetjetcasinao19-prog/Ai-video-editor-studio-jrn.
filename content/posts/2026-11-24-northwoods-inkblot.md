- **Fact check:**
  - Source: Joint Chiefs of Staff, memorandum for the Secretary of Defense,
    "Justification for US Military Intervention in Cuba (TS)," 13 March 1962, with the
    annex from the Northwoods planning group. Declassified in 1997 by the Assassination
    Records Review Board; available from the National Security Archive (George
    Washington University). Popularized by James Bamford, *Body of Secrets* (2001).
  - The memo was signed by JCS Chairman Gen. Lyman Lemnitzer, and the Joint Chiefs
    recommended the proposals be approved and sent up the chain. "America's top generals
    approved a plan" means they endorsed it, not that it was authorized or carried out.
  - The proposals included a "Communist Cuban terror campaign in the Miami area" and in
    Washington, blowing up a US ship in Guantánamo Bay and blaming Cuba, and staging an
    incident to make it look as if Cuba shot down a chartered civilian airliner — in that
    plan an empty drone would be swapped in for the real plane, whose passengers would
    land safely.
  - Secretary of Defense Robert McNamara and the Kennedy administration rejected it.
    None of it was carried out.
  - Please don't use this case as "proof" that other real events were staged. Northwoods
    is documented because the memo itself survived; claims about other events need
    their own evidence.
