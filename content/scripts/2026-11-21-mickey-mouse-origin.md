## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Mickey Mouse was born…
because Walt Disney lost his biggest star.

1928. Walt Disney has a hit cartoon: Oswald the Lucky Rabbit.
In New York, he learns Universal owns Oswald…
and most of his animators are leaving him.

On the train home, he needs a new star.
With his loyal animator Ub Iwerks, he creates… a mouse.
Then Walt tries something new: a cartoon with sound.

November 1928. "Steamboat Willie" opens in New York.
Mickey whistles. The crowd loves it.

And in 2024, that very first Mickey entered the public domain.

A lost rabbit… gave the world its most famous mouse.

Every legend has a beginning.

## Visuals — where to get them
- Steamboat Willie (1928) is public domain in the US since Jan 1, 2024, so
  short clips of that film can be used in a US video — but NOT later Mickey
  designs, the Disney logo, or anything that suggests Disney made or endorses
  your video (Disney still holds trademarks). Check the clip's source is the
  1928 film itself.
- Oswald the Lucky Rabbit is owned by Disney again today: don't show him; use
  a plain rabbit silhouette instead.
- Pictures: Prompt Studio → free pictures (Pollinations.ai) or Google AI
  (Nano Banana), e.g. "1928 night train crossing the desert, a man in a suit
  sketching at a window seat, seen from behind, warm film photo, vertical 9:16".
  Don't make AI faces of Walt Disney or Ub Iwerks.
