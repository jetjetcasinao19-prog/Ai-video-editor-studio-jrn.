> One word can change what you remember. Manipulators know it.
>
> In 1974, Elizabeth Loftus showed people a car crash video.
> Then she asked how fast the cars were going when they "hit"... or "smashed" into each other.
>
> A week later: did you see broken glass? There was none.

> "Smashed": 32% said yes. "Hit": 14%.
