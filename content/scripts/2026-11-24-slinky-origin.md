A falling spring… became a classic American toy.

1943. Pennsylvania.
Naval engineer Richard James is working with springs.
As the story goes, one falls… and keeps "walking."
He tells his wife, Betty: this could be a toy.
Betty flips through a dictionary for a name.
She picks "slinky": sleek and graceful.

November 1945. Philadelphia's Gimbels department store.
They have 400 Slinkys.
In 90 minutes… every one is sold.
