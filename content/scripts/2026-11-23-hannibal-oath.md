## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Rome's greatest enemy made his promise…
when he was nine years old.

Carthage, North Africa.
Hannibal begs his father, the general Hamilcar, to take him to war.
As Hannibal later told it, his father agreed on one condition:
swear never to be a friend of Rome.
He keeps that promise.

218 BC. Now a general, Hannibal marches an army from Spain
over the snowy Alps into Italy… with dozens of war elephants.
Many soldiers and elephants don't survive the crossing.
Two years later, at Cannae,
he crushes a Roman army about twice the size of his own.
But he never takes the city of Rome itself.

A child's promise… that nearly brought Rome to its knees.

Every legend has a beginning.
