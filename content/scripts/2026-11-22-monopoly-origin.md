## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Monopoly was invented…
to show how unfair monopolies are.

1904. Lizzie Magie patents a board game called "The Landlord's Game."
Players buy property, charge rent, and watch others go broke.
She wanted people to see how unfair that is.

For years, people make their own homemade copies.
Players in Atlantic City add their own street names.

Then, in the 1930s, a man named Charles Darrow
sells a version to Parker Brothers.
He's credited as the inventor.
Royalties make him a millionaire.
Lizzie? Parker Brothers buys her patent for 500 dollars.

The truth only comes out in the 1970s,
during a court fight over another board game.

A game about unfairness… with an unfair origin story.

Every legend has a beginning.

## Visuals — where to get them
- Real images: Lizzie Magie's 1904 patent drawing is a US government document
  (public domain) — find it on Google Patents (US748626) or Wikimedia Commons.
- Film it yourself (free hook): roll dice on any generic board, sweep play
  money off the table.
- Don't show the Monopoly box, board design, "Mr. Monopoly" mascot, Hasbro or
  Parker Brothers logos — they're trademarks. Use generic boards and money.
- Pictures: Prompt Studio → free pictures (Pollinations.ai) or Google AI
  (Nano Banana), e.g. "1904 Victorian parlor, a woman drawing a square board
  game at a table, seen from behind, warm film photo, vertical 9:16". Don't
  make AI faces of Lizzie Magie or Charles Darrow.
