## Voice-over script (read aloud)
In 1974, farmers digging a well in China hit something strange.

March 1974. A village near Xi'an. A drought.
Yang Zhifa and his brothers are digging for water.
Instead, they find pieces of a life-size clay soldier.
Archaeologists arrive.
And they realize what it is.

Buried for over 2,000 years… is an army.
An estimated 8,000 clay soldiers, plus horses and war chariots.
Made for China's first emperor, Qin Shi Huang, to guard him after death.
Each soldier has its own face.
Generals stand tallest.
All this time, the army was guarding him…
right under the farmers' fields.

A search for water… that uncovered an emperor's army.

Every legend has a beginning.
