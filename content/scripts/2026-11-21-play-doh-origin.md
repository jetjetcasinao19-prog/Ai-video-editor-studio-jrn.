## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Play-Doh was invented… to clean your walls.

1930s. Cincinnati, Ohio.
Homes burn coal for heat, and the soot turns wallpaper black.
A soap company called Kutol sells a soft, doughy putty
that rolls the dirt right off.

Then, after World War Two, homes switch from coal to gas and oil.
Washable wallpaper arrives.
Nobody needs the putty anymore.

Then Kay Zufall, a nursery school teacher in the family,
reads that kids are modeling with wallpaper cleaner.
She tries it with her class.
The kids love it.

Her idea? Sell it as a toy.
In 1956, it's reborn with a new name: Play-Doh.

A wall cleaner… that became a childhood classic.

Every legend has a beginning.
