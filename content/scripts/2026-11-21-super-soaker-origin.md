## Voice-over script (read aloud)
A rocket scientist invented this toy… by accident.
In his bathroom.

1982. Lonnie Johnson, an engineer who worked on NASA missions,
is building a cooling system that runs on water.
He tests a homemade nozzle on his bathroom sink.
Whoosh. A blast of water shoots across the room.
This would make an amazing water gun.

He builds a prototype.
But it takes years to get a toy company on board.
He keeps pushing.
Finally, a company called Larami says yes.

In 1990, it hits stores as the Power Drencher.
Then it gets a new name: the Super Soaker.
In three years, Larami sells about 27 million of them.

A science experiment… that soaked a whole generation.

Every legend has a beginning.
