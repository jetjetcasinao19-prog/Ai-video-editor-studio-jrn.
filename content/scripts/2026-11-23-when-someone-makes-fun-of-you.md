## Full voice-over (read this)
> Someone's making fun of you? Don't fight back. Do this.
>
> Therapists call it "fogging." It comes from a 1975 book, *When I Say No, I Feel Guilty*.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Calmly agree with any part that's true.
>
> "Nice shoes, grandpa." "Yeah, they're comfy."
> "You're always late." "True, I was late today."
>
> No anger. No defending.
> Like fog: they throw punches, and nothing lands.
>
> Why it works? A tease wants a reaction.
> Give it nothing, and it has nothing to grab.
>
> One thing: this is for teasing.
> If it's bullying, or it keeps happening, tell someone you trust.
>
> Follow for more mind tricks.
