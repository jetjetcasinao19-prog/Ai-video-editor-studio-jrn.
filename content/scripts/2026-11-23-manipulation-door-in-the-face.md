> Manipulators don't start with what they want. They start with something crazy.
>
> In 1975, psychologist Robert Cialdini asked students to take teens from a detention center to the zoo.
> Only 17% said yes.
>
> So he asked others something huge first: volunteer two hours a week, for two years.
> Everyone said no.
> Then: "Okay... could you just do the zoo trip?"
> Now 50% said yes.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> When they back down, you feel you should meet them halfway.
>
> So when a big ask suddenly shrinks...
> ask yourself: would I say yes to this one alone?
