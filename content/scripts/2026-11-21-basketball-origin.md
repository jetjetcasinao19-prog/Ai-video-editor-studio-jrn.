## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Basketball started with… two peach baskets.

1891. Springfield, Massachusetts.
It's winter. Too cold to play outside.
A young gym teacher, James Naismith, gets a challenge from his boss:
invent an indoor game to keep his students active.
Fun fact: Naismith wasn't even American. He was Canadian.

He grabs a soccer ball.
He nails two peach baskets ten feet up.
He writes 13 rules.
Then the class plays the very first game.

December 21st. Nine players a side.
And the final score? One to nothing.
Plus, in those early games, every time someone scored,
a janitor had to climb a ladder to get the ball back.

Those 13 rules… became one of the most popular sports on Earth.

Every legend has a beginning.
