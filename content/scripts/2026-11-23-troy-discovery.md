## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The man famous for finding Troy…
may have destroyed the real Troy.

For centuries, people think the Trojan War is just a myth from Homer.
Then a British official, Frank Calvert, becomes convinced
Troy lies under a hill in Turkey called Hisarlik.

In 1870, a rich German businessman, Heinrich Schliemann, arrives…
on Calvert's advice.
He cuts a giant trench straight through the hill,
tossing away layers he thinks are too young.
In 1873, he announces "Priam's Treasure,"
gold of Troy's legendary king.
But it's from a layer about 1,000 years older than Homer's Troy.
And he smuggles it out of the country.

A legend found… and nearly ruined by its hunter.

Every legend has a beginning.
