## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Nintendo is 137 years old.
And it started with… playing cards.

1889. Kyoto, Japan.
Fusajiro Yamauchi opens a small shop making Japanese playing cards.

Decades later, a new boss, Hiroshi Yamauchi, wants more than cards.
Nintendo tries a taxi company. Instant rice.
None of it lasts.
By the mid-1960s, Nintendo is close to going broke.

Then the boss visits a card factory
and spots a young engineer, Gunpei Yokoi, playing with an arm he built for fun.
He tells him: turn it into a toy.
The Ultra Hand sells over a million.

And that engineer?
He later led the team behind the Game Boy.

A card shop… that became a gaming giant.

Every legend has a beginning.
