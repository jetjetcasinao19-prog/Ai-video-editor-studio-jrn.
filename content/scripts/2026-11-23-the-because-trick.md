## Full voice-over (read this)
> One word makes people say yes. And it's not "please."
>
> In 1978, psychologist Ellen Langer tested it at a busy copy machine.
> Strangers asked to cut in line. Three different ways.
>
> First: "I have five pages. May I use the copy machine?"
> Sixty percent said yes.
>
> Second: "...because I'm in a rush?"
> Ninety-four percent said yes.
>
> Third: "...because I have to make copies?"
> Of course you do. It's a copy machine.
> Still... ninety-three percent said yes.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Your brain runs on autopilot. It hears "because"...
> and assumes a good reason is coming. So it stops checking.
>
> But there's a catch. For twenty pages, the empty "because" did nothing.
>
> Small favor? Say "because."
> Big favor? Bring a real reason.
>
> Follow for more mind tricks.
