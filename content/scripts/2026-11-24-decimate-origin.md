## Voice-over script (read aloud)
"Decimate" didn't always mean destroy almost everything.
It meant something much darker.

In ancient Rome, if an army unit disgraced itself,
it could face "decimatio."
The soldiers were split into groups of ten.
They drew lots.
One in every ten was killed… by the other nine.
It was so harsh, Rome rarely used it.

Today, "decimate" means wiping out most of something.
Originally, it meant just one in ten.

Every legend has a beginning.
