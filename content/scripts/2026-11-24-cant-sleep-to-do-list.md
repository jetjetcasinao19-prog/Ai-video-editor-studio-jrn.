## Full voice-over (read this)
> Can't fall asleep because your brain won't stop? Do this.
>
> In 2018, Baylor University researchers brought people into a sleep lab.
> Before bed, half wrote a to-do list for tomorrow. Half wrote what they'd already done.
>
> The to-do list group fell asleep about 9 minutes faster.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Your brain keeps replaying unfinished tasks.
> Writing them down tells it: it's handled.
>
> Five minutes. Pen and paper. Be specific.
>
> Follow for more mind tricks.
