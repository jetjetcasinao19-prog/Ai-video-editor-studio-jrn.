## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The Times Square ball drop exists…
because fireworks got banned.

1904. New York.
The New York Times moves into a brand-new tower.
The area gets renamed Times Square.
To celebrate New Year's Eve, the newspaper's owner, Adolph Ochs,
sets off fireworks from the roof.
Huge crowds show up.
Then the city bans the fireworks.

So Ochs needs a new idea.
He borrows one from sailors.
For decades, ships set their clocks
by watching "time balls" drop from towers at an exact time.

On December 31st, 1907,
a 700-pound ball of iron and wood, covered in 100 light bulbs,
drops at midnight.

A party trick… now watched by millions every year.

Every legend has a beginning.
