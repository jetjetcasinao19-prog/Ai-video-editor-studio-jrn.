## Full voice-over (read this)
> Craving a snack? Eat it... in your head. Thirty times.
>
> In 2010, Carnegie Mellon researchers had people imagine eating M&M's, one by one.
> Then they got a real bowl.
>
> People who pictured eating 30 ate fewer real ones.
> Picturing just 3? Didn't help.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Your brain gets tired of a food just by imagining it, bite after bite.
>
> So next craving: picture every bite. Slowly.
>
> Follow for more mind tricks.
