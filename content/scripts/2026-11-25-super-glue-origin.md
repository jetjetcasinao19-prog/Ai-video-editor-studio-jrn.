## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Super Glue was a failure… at first.

1942. World War Two.
Chemist Harry Coover at Kodak is trying to make clear plastic for gun sights.
One material sticks to everything it touches.
Too sticky. It gets tossed aside.

Years later, his team tests it again…
and it glues expensive lab equipment together.
That's when Coover realizes: this isn't a failure.
It's a super glue.
In 1958, it goes on sale as "Eastman 910."

Every legend has a beginning.
