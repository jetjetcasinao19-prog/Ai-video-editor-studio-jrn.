## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The stethoscope was invented… because a doctor felt awkward.

1816. Paris.
Doctor René Laennec has a young female patient with heart trouble.
Back then, doctors listened by pressing an ear right against the chest.
Laennec thinks that's improper.
So he rolls a sheet of paper into a tube… and listens through it.
He can hear her heart better than ever.

He builds a wooden version and calls it the stethoscope:
Greek for "chest" and "examine."

Every legend has a beginning.
