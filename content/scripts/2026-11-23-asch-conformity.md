> In the 1950s, psychologist Solomon Asch showed people three lines.

> One by one, they all gave the same wrong answer.
> Three out of four real people went along at least once.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Your brain hates standing out.

> And if someone stands alone? Stand with them.
