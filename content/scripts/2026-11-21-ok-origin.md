## Voice-over script (read aloud)
OK is one of the most used words on Earth.
And it started… as a joke.

1830s. Boston.
Newspapers have a trend:
joke abbreviations of misspelled words.
On March 23rd, 1839, the Boston Morning Post prints "O.K."…
short for "oll korrect."
Spelled wrong on purpose.

Most of these jokes died fast. OK didn't.

In 1840, President Martin Van Buren runs for reelection.
His nickname? Old Kinderhook, after his hometown in New York.
His fans form O.K. Clubs.
Van Buren loses. But the word wins.

In the 1960s, a language researcher traced it back to that one newspaper joke.

A silly spelling joke… that the whole world now says.

Every legend has a beginning.
