## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The doctor who told other doctors to wash their hands… was rejected.

1847. Vienna.
Doctor Ignaz Semmelweis notices something terrible.
New mothers treated by doctors die of a fever
far more often than those treated by midwives.
The doctors often come straight from doing autopsies.
So he orders them to wash their hands in a chlorine solution.
Deaths drop sharply.

But many doctors refuse to believe they're the problem.
Semmelweis dies in 1865…
years before germs are widely accepted.
