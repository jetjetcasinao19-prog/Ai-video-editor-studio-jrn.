## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Teflon was discovered… inside a tank that seemed empty.

April 6th, 1938. New Jersey.
DuPont chemist Roy Plunkett is working on a new gas for refrigerators.
He opens a tank of it.
Nothing comes out.
But the tank still weighs as much as a full one.
So they cut it open.
Inside: a slippery white, waxy powder.
The gas had turned into a brand-new plastic.
By 1941, it has a name: Teflon.

Every legend has a beginning.
