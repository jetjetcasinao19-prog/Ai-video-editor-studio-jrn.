## Voice-over script (read aloud)
In the middle of World War One…
enemies stopped fighting for Christmas.

December 1914. The Western Front.
British and German soldiers face each other
from muddy, freezing trenches.
Earlier that month, the Pope asked for a Christmas truce.
Leaders said no.

But on Christmas Eve, the British hear something from the German side…
singing. "Silent Night."
Soon, soldiers climb out and meet in no man's land,
the deadly ground between the trenches.
They shake hands. Share food. Swap gifts.
Bury their dead together.
In some places, they even play soccer.

The generals are furious.
They order it never to happen again.
It never happens on that scale again.

One silent night… when enemies chose peace.

Every legend has a beginning.
