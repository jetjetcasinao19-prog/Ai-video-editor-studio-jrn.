## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Penicillin started with… a messy lab bench.

September 1928. London.
Scientist Alexander Fleming comes back from vacation.
A dish of bacteria he left out is covered in mold.
But around the mold, the bacteria are dead.
The mold is making something that kills germs.
He calls it penicillin.

But for years, it stays in the lab.
Around 1940, a team at Oxford, led by Howard Florey and Ernst Chain,
finally turns it into a real drug.

Every legend has a beginning.
