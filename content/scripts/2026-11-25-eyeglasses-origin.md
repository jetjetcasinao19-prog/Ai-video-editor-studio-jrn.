## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Nobody knows who invented eyeglasses.
But we know roughly when.

Around 1306, an Italian friar named Giordano gives a sermon in Florence.
He says glasses were invented less than twenty years ago…
and that he met the man who made them.
He never tells us his name.

So the first glasses came from Italy, around the 1280s…
probably in Pisa or Florence.

The person who helped millions see clearly… is still a mystery.

Every legend has a beginning.
