## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The Nike swoosh cost… 35 dollars.

1971. Portland, Oregon.
Phil Knight is teaching accounting at Portland State University.
His shoe company needs a logo. Fast.
He hires a graphic design student, Carolyn Davidson.
She sketches idea after idea.
One looks like motion… a simple, curved stroke.

Knight's reaction?
"I don't love it… but maybe it will grow on me."
She sends him a bill for 35 dollars.

The swoosh goes on the shoes.
The company becomes Nike…
one of the biggest sports brands on Earth.

In 1983, Knight surprises her with a gold swoosh ring with a diamond…
and shares of Nike stock.

A 35-dollar sketch… now one of the most famous logos ever.

Every legend has a beginning.

## Visuals — where to get them
- Film it yourself (free hook): draw your own curved stroke on paper with a
  marker; write "$35" next to it.
- Pictures: Prompt Studio → free pictures (Pollinations.ai) or Google AI
  (Nano Banana), e.g. "1971 university art room, student at a drafting table
  sketching shoe logos, seen from behind, warm film photo, vertical 9:16".
  Don't make AI faces of Phil Knight or Carolyn Davidson.
- Trademark: the Swoosh and the Nike name are trademarks. Talking about them in
  a history video is fine, but don't put the real logo as a big graphic, on
  your thumbnail, or next to your own brand — use a hand-drawn curve instead.
