## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The woman who wrote "Mary Had a Little Lamb"…
is the reason you get Thanksgiving.
Her name? Sarah Josepha Hale.

In the 1800s, Thanksgiving was a mess.
Every state picked its own day.
Many didn't celebrate it at all.

Sarah ran one of America's most popular magazines.
She used it to push for one national day of thanks.
She wrote to presidents.
Taylor. Fillmore. Pierce. Buchanan.
None of them acted.

She didn't quit.

1863. The country is torn apart by the Civil War.
She writes one more letter… to Abraham Lincoln.
Days later, Lincoln declares a national Thanksgiving.
The last Thursday in November.

One woman, one pen… and a holiday the whole country shares.

Every legend has a beginning.
