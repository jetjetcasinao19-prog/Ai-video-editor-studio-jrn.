## Voice-over script (read aloud)
"Crossing the Rubicon" means a point of no return.
It started with a small river… and a big crime.

49 BC. Julius Caesar has conquered Gaul.
Now the Senate orders him to give up his army.
Bringing an army into Italy is against the law.
The border? A small river called the Rubicon.
In January, Caesar leads a single legion to its banks… and crosses.
According to the writer Suetonius, he says: "The die is cast."

It's treason. Rome's civil war begins.
Caesar wins, and becomes dictator.
Rome changes forever.
Today, historians still aren't sure exactly which river was the Rubicon.

One small stream… that became a phrase for no turning back.

Every legend has a beginning.
