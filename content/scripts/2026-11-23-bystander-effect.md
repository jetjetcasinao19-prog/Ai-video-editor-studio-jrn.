## Full voice-over (read this)
> If you ever need help in a crowd... don't yell "Somebody help!"
>
> In 1968, Columbia psychologists filled a room with smoke.
> It poured in through a vent.
>
> When people were alone, 75% went to report it.
> But with two strangers who just kept working... only 10% did.
>
> They sat in the smoke. Coughing. Rubbing their eyes.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> It's called the bystander effect.
> In a crowd, everyone waits for someone else.
>
> So point at one person.
> "You, in the red shirt. Call 911."
> Now it's not "somebody." It's them.
>
