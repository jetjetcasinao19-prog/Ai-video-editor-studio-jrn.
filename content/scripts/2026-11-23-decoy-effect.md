>
> The Economist once offered three subscriptions.
> Web only: $59. Print only: $125. Print and web: also $125.
> Who'd pick print only? Nobody.
>
> A behavioral economist tested it on 100 MIT students.
> Zero chose print only. 84 chose the $125 combo.
>
> Then he removed the useless option.
> Now only 32 chose the combo. Most took the cheap one.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> The middle option was a decoy.
> It made the combo look like a free upgrade.
>
> Next time you see three prices... ask which one is there just to push you.
