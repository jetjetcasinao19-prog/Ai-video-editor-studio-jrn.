## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Coca-Cola made Santa red?
That's a myth.

1823. A poem called "A Visit from St. Nicholas"
describes him as jolly and plump,
flying a sleigh with eight reindeer.

In the 1860s, cartoonist Thomas Nast starts drawing Santa for Harper's Weekly.
Over the years, his Santa's coat turns red.
Long before Coke, other artists were painting Santa in red, too.

Then, in 1931, Coca-Cola hires artist Haddon Sundblom
to paint Santa for its ads.
His warm, smiling Santa runs for over 30 years…
and the world falls in love.

Even Coca-Cola says it didn't invent him.
The red? That's just luck.

A poem, a cartoonist, and a soda ad… built the Santa we know.

Every legend has a beginning.
