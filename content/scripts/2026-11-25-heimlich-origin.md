## Voice-over script (read aloud)
In 1974, a doctor published a trick for saving choking people…
that had only been tested on dogs.
Dr. Henry Heimlich wrote about pushing air up from the lungs
to pop the food out.
His article came out in June 1974.
Within weeks, a newspaper in the Seattle area reported
someone had already used it to save a choking person.
Soon, people called it the Heimlich maneuver.

A trick tested on dogs… now taught around the world.

Every legend has a beginning.
