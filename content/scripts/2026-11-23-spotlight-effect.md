## Next topic ideas (same niche, research before scripting)
1. **The bystander effect** — the smoke-filled room: alone, most people reported the smoke; with calm strangers, few did (Latané & Darley, 1968).
2. **The peak-end rule** — people chose to repeat a *longer* cold-water test because it ended less painfully (Kahneman et al., 1993).
3. **The Stroop test** — an interactive one: "Say the color, not the word." Your brain can't stop reading.
4. **The mere exposure effect** — you like things more just because you've seen them more (Zajonc, 1968).
5. **The pratfall effect** — a skilled person who spills coffee becomes more likable (Aronson, 1966).
