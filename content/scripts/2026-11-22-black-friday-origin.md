## Voice-over script (read aloud)
You've heard Black Friday means stores finally make a profit.
That's not where the name came from.

Back in the early 1960s, in Philadelphia,
the day after Thanksgiving is total chaos.
Shoppers flood downtown.
The Army-Navy football game is that weekend too.
Traffic jams everywhere.

Police officers have to work long shifts.
They give the day a grumpy nickname: Black Friday.
Store owners hate it.
They try to rebrand it "Big Friday."
It doesn't stick.

By the 1980s, a happier story appears:
Black Friday is when stores go from red ink… to black.
A nice story. Just not the original one.

A cop's complaint… that became one of America's biggest shopping days.

Every legend has a beginning.
