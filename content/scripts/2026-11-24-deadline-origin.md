## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Your next deadline won't kill you.
But the first ones could.

1860s. The American Civil War.
In prison camps like Andersonville, Georgia,
guards marked a line near the walls.
Usually a low fence.
Cross it… and you could be shot.
They called it the "deadline."

Later, printers and newspapers borrowed the word…
until it meant a time limit.
Now it just means: get it done.

Every legend has a beginning.
