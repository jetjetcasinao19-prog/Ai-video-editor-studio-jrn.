## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The first person to see bacteria… wasn't a scientist.

1670s. Delft, Netherlands.
Antonie van Leeuwenhoek works in the cloth trade.
He grinds his own tiny glass lenses and builds simple microscopes.
In water, he sees tiny moving creatures.
He calls them "animalcules": little animals.
In 1683, he scrapes the plaque from his own teeth…
and finds it full of them.
He mails his discoveries to London's Royal Society,
letter after letter, for 50 years.

Every legend has a beginning.
