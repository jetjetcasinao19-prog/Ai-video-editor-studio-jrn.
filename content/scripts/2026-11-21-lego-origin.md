## Voice-over script (read aloud)
LEGO started with…
a carpenter who was running out of work.

1932. Billund, Denmark.
The Great Depression hits.
Work dries up for Ole Kirk Christiansen.
So he starts carving wooden toys instead.
Wooden ducks. Yo-yos.

In 1934, he names his company LEGO,
from the Danish words "leg godt."
Meaning: play well.

Then, in 1942, his factory burns to the ground.
He rebuilds.

In 1949, the company starts making a new kind of toy:
plastic bricks that stack together, inspired by a British toy.
In 1958, they patent the brick we know today,
with tubes inside that make it click and hold.

A carpenter's side project… now found in homes around the world.

Every legend has a beginning.
