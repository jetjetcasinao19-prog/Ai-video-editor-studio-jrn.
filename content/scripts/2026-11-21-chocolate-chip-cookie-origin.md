## Voice-over script (read aloud)
You've heard the chocolate chip cookie was an accident.
It wasn't.

1930s. Whitman, Massachusetts.
Ruth Wakefield runs a popular restaurant called the Toll House Inn.
She's a trained dietitian.
She knows exactly what she's doing.

She chops up a bar of Nestlé chocolate
and mixes the pieces into her cookie dough.
The cookies are a hit.
In 1938, she puts the recipe in her cookbook.

Then Nestlé makes her a deal:
the recipe and the Toll House name, printed on their packaging.
Her payment? One dollar… which she said she never got.
And free chocolate for life.

Soon, Nestlé starts selling chocolate chips.

A chef's plan… that became an American classic.

Every legend has a beginning.
