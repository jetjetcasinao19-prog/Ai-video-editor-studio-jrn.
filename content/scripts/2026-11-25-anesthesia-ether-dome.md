## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Before 1846, surgery meant being awake… for every second.

October 16th, 1846. Boston. Massachusetts General Hospital.
Dentist William Morton has a patient, Gilbert Abbott, breathe in ether.
Surgeon John Collins Warren operates on a tumor in his neck…
while Abbott sleeps.
Warren turns to the crowd:
"Gentlemen, this is no humbug."

The room becomes known as the Ether Dome…
the start of modern anesthesia.

Every legend has a beginning.
