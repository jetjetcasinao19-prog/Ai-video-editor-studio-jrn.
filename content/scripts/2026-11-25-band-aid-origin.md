## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The Band-Aid was invented… out of love.

Around 1920. New Jersey.
Earle Dickson works for Johnson & Johnson.
His wife, Josephine, keeps cutting and burning her fingers in the kitchen.
Regular bandages fall right off.
So Earle puts small squares of gauze down a long strip of tape…
so she can cut one off and fix herself, even when he's not home.
His boss loves it.
In 1921, Band-Aids go on sale.

Every legend has a beginning.
