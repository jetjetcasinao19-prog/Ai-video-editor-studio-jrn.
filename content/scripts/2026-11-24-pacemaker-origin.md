## Voice-over script (read aloud)
One of the first implantable pacemakers started with… the wrong part.

1950s. Buffalo, New York.
Engineer Wilson Greatbatch is building a device to record heartbeats.
He installs the wrong part by mistake.
Instead of recording, the circuit starts to pulse…
steady, like a heartbeat.
He realizes: this could keep a weak heart beating.
With surgeon William Chardack,
he builds a tiny pacemaker that fits inside the body.
In 1960, their pacemaker is implanted in a patient.
