## Full voice-over (read this)
> Nervous before something big? Don't say "calm down." Say this.
>
> In 2014, Harvard researcher Alison Wood Brooks had people sing karaoke, scored by a computer.
>
> Before singing, some said "I am anxious." Others said "I am excited."
> The "excited" group scored way higher.
>
> She found the same thing with public speaking and math tests.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Nerves and excitement feel the same in your body.
> Racing heart. Fast breathing.
> Trying to calm down fights your body. Calling it excitement uses it.
>
> So next time, say it out loud: "I'm excited."
>
> Follow for more mind tricks.
