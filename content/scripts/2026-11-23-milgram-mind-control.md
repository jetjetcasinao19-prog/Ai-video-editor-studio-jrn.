## Full voice-over (read this)
> The closest thing to real mind control? A man in a lab coat.
>
> In 1961, Yale psychologist Stanley Milgram told volunteers to shock a stranger for every wrong answer.
> 15 volts. Then 30. All the way to 450.
>
> He banged on the wall. Then went silent.
> The man in the lab coat said: "Please continue."
>
> Students predicted about 1% would go all the way. 65% did.
>
> The shocks were fake. The stranger was an actor. They didn't know.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Authority, plus tiny steps.
> Each shock was just a little worse than the last.
>
> The antidote? When others refused, far fewer obeyed.
> So be the first to say no.
>
> Follow for more mind tricks.
