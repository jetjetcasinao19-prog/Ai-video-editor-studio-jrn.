## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The Great Wall of China isn't one wall.
And it wasn't built at once.

Around the 600s BC, China is split into rival kingdoms at war.
They build walls against each other.
In 221 BC, one kingdom, Qin, conquers them all.
Its ruler becomes China's first emperor.
He connects many old walls into one system,
to keep out raiders from the north.
The twist? Some of those walls were built to keep the Qin out.

Most of the wall you see in photos today
was built much later, by the Ming dynasty.
And no, you can't see it from space with just your eyes.

A patchwork of enemy walls… that became a symbol of a nation.

Every legend has a beginning.
