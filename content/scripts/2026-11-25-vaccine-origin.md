## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The word "vaccine" comes from… a cow.

1700s England.
Doctor Edward Jenner hears that milkmaids who catch cowpox,
a mild disease from cows, don't seem to get deadly smallpox.
May 1796.
He takes material from a milkmaid's cowpox sore…
and puts it into a small cut on an 8-year-old boy, James Phipps.
Weeks later, he exposes the boy to smallpox.
James doesn't get sick.

The Latin for cow is "vacca."
So we call it… vaccination.

Every legend has a beginning.
