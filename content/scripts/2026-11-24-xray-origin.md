## Voice-over script (read aloud)
X-rays were discovered… by accident, in a dark lab.

November 8th, 1895. Germany.
Physicist Wilhelm Röntgen covers a glass tube in black cardboard
and switches on the current.
Across the room, a special screen starts to glow.
Something invisible is passing through the cardboard.
He doesn't know what it is…
so he calls it "X," for unknown.
Weeks later, he takes a picture of his wife's hand.
You can see her bones… and her wedding ring.

Every legend has a beginning.
