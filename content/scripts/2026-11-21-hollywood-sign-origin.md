## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The Hollywood sign was supposed to come down after a year and a half.

1923. Los Angeles.
Developers are selling homes in the hills,
in a neighborhood called Hollywoodland.
To get attention, they put up a giant ad on the hillside.
Huge white letters, lit up at night.

But it never comes down.

Years later, it's falling apart.
In 1949, the Hollywood Chamber of Commerce repairs it…
and removes the word "land."

By the 1970s, it's a wreck.
So celebrities step in.
Each donor pays for one letter.
Rock star Alice Cooper pays for an O.
In 1978, a brand-new sign goes up.

A real estate ad… that became the symbol of the movies.

Every legend has a beginning.
