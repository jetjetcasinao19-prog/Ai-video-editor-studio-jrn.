## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The best comeback in history… was one word.

Sparta was in a region of Greece called Laconia.
Spartans were famous for saying a lot… with very few words.
As the story goes, King Philip the Second, father of Alexander the Great,
sends them a threat:
if I invade your land, I will destroy your city.
The Spartans reply with one word: "If."

That's where we get the word "laconic":
short, sharp, and to the point.

Every legend has a beginning.
