> Song stuck in your head? Grab a piece of gum.
>
> In 2015, psychologists at the University of Reading played people catchy pop songs.
> Then they asked them not to think about them.
>
> People chewing gum heard the song in their heads less often.
>
> **Here's the trick.**
> Replaying a song uses your inner voice, like silently singing.
> Chewing jams that system. Less room for the chorus.
>
> So next time it's on repeat... chew.
>
