## Voice-over script (read aloud)
Everyone remembers the 300 Spartans.
But they weren't alone.

480 BC. Persian King Xerxes invades Greece with a massive army.
About 7,000 Greeks block a narrow mountain pass called Thermopylae.
Only 300 of them are Spartans, led by King Leonidas.
For three days, the pass holds.

Then a local man named Ephialtes
shows the Persians a secret path around the mountains.
The Greeks are about to be surrounded.
Leonidas sends most of the army away.

But the Spartans stay…
and so do about 700 soldiers from a small city called Thespiae.
They fight to the very end.
History mostly remembers the Spartans.

A defeat… that became the most famous last stand in history.

Every legend has a beginning.
