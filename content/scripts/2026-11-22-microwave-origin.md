## Voice-over script (read aloud)
The microwave oven was invented…
because of a melted candy bar.

1940s. Massachusetts.
Percy Spencer, an engineer who never went past grammar school,
is working on radar at Raytheon.
He's standing next to a magnetron, the tube that powers radar.
Then he notices… the candy bar in his pocket has melted.

Most people would shrug. He gets curious.
He aims the tube at an egg.
It explodes.
He realizes these waves can cook food.

Raytheon patents it, and the first microwave oven goes on sale:
almost six feet tall, about 750 pounds.
His reward for the patent?
The same two-dollar bonus Raytheon gave every inventor on staff.

A melted snack… now in kitchens everywhere.

Every legend has a beginning.
