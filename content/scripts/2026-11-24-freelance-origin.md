## Voice-over script (read aloud)
"Freelancer" sounds modern.
It actually means a soldier for hire… with a lance.

Mercenaries fought for whoever paid them.
But the word itself?
Dictionaries trace it to a novel:
"Ivanhoe," by Sir Walter Scott, around 1820.
A lord brags about his hired knights: his "free lances."
Free, because they weren't loyal to any lord.

Later, it meant independent politicians…
then anyone who works for themselves.

Every legend has a beginning.
