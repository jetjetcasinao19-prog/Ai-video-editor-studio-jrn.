ANIMATED STICK FIGURE VIDEOS • ← back to Video Studio • AI Story Studio

01 — Story (AI Director)

Paste your narration, one or more sentences. The director picks a stickman action for every sentence (walking, ideas, talking, celebrating…). Works offline on the phone.

02 — Script

One shot per line: seconds action: words, for example 3 wave: Hi, I'm Sticky!. Seconds are optional. Lines starting with // are notes. Tap an action to add a line:

03 — Look

04 — Narration voice

An AI voice reads every line of your script, right on your phone. The first time it downloads the voice model (about 92 MB, use Wi-Fi); after that it works offline. Shots get longer automatically so the voice always fits, and the voice is saved inside the exported video.

05 — Preview

06 — Export

Records the animation in real time (a 30 s video takes 30 s). Keep this screen open. Then open the video in Video Studio to add music, voice-over or more captions.
